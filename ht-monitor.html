HT Monitor Tool – Documentation

Lists active Home Telehealth patients for the selected facility and HT Care Coordinator. Data refreshes nightly (simulated: as of 6/29/2026 12AM, recalculated each time you view the report).

Next Appt / Next Appt Clinic
Earliest future appointment in any clinic, from VistA Scheduling.
Next Appts Count
Number of future appointments.
180 Day Note
Most recent signed HT assessment, treatment plan or periodic evaluation note in the last 180 days.
COC Status / COC Date
Home Telehealth category of care and status from the VistA HT patient file, and the date of the last activation or category of care change.
Past 3 HT Interventions
Dates of the last three checked-out appointments in Home Telehealth clinics.
HT Monthly Note
Date of the most recent signed HOME TELEHEALTH CARE COORDINATION NOTE (monthly contact).
L2Note
Most recent signed Home Telehealth note of any title.
Days Since HT Note
Days from the most recent signed HT note to the report date. Shown in red when more than 30 days.
